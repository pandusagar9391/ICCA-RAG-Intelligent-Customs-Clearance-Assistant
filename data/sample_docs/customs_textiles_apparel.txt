# CUSTOMS CLEARANCE REGULATIONS FOR TEXTILES AND APPAREL IMPORTS

## 1. REGULATORY OVERVIEW

India's textile sector — encompassing cotton, synthetic fibres, wool, silk, and man-made fibre products — is one of the most import-sensitive industries. While India is a major textile exporter, specific categories of finished garments, technical textiles, and specialty fibres are imported under a carefully regulated framework involving Customs (CBIC), the Textile Commissioner, and in several cases anti-dumping and countervailing duty orders.

Key governing statutes:
1. **Customs Act, 1962** — primary import regulation.
2. **Textile Committee Act, 1963** — mandates Textile Committee testing and certification for specified textile imports.
3. **Foreign Trade Policy (FTP)** — Import policy classification (Free/Restricted/Prohibited) for textile HS headings.
4. **BIS Act, 2016** — mandatory quality standards for certain technical textiles.
5. **Anti-Dumping Rules, 1995** (under Customs Tariff Act, 1975) — provides for imposition of Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD) on dumped textile imports.

## 3. TEXTILE COMMITTEE REGISTRATION AND TESTING

### 3.1 Textile Committee (TC) Mandatory Inspection
Under the Textile Committee Act, 1963 and the Textile Committee (Inspection of Textile and Textile Machinery) Regulations, specified categories of imported textiles require mandatory pre-shipment or port-of-entry inspection and certification by the **Textile Committee (TC)**, Ministry of Textiles.

**Categories requiring TC inspection**:
- Silk and silk blended fabrics (HS 5007) — to prevent mislabelling of synthetic blends as pure silk.
- Wool and wool blended fabrics (HS 5111, 5112) — composition testing for declared wool percentage.
- Cotton fabrics (HS 5208–5212) — thread count, weight per square metre verification.
- Man-made fibre fabrics — quality and composition verification.
- Readymade garments (HS 6101–6117, HS 6201–6217) — fabric composition, sizing, care label compliance.

### 3.2 Textile Research and Registration Certificate (TRC)
The importing entity must obtain a **Textile Research and Registration Certificate (TRC)** from the Textile Committee:
1. **Pre-Shipment Inspection**: The overseas manufacturer or the Indian importer arranges TC-empanelled inspection agency to test fabric samples at origin.
2. **Port-of-Entry Testing**: Upon arrival, the consignment is sampled at the customs examination shed and samples are tested at Textile Committee laboratories in Mumbai (Worli), Ahmedabad, Coimbatore, or Kolkata.
3. **Quality Certification**: TC issues quality certification confirming the imported textile meets declared composition, weight, and dimensional stability standards.
4. **Mismatch Penalty**: If the actual composition differs from the declared composition by more than the permitted tolerance (typically ±3% for fibre content), the consignment is detained and misdeclaration penalty is levied.

## 4. BIS QUALITY CONTROL FOR TECHNICAL TEXTILES

Certain technical textile products are notified under BIS Quality Control Orders (QCO):
- **Safety Footwear** (HS 6403/6405): BIS IS 15298 mandatory certification.
- **High Visibility Clothing** (HS 6210): BIS IS 15809 mandatory certification for reflective safety garments.
- **Jute Products** (HS 5305, 5310): Jute Commissioner's NOC for specified jute product imports.
- **Nonwoven fabrics** used in medical/hygiene applications (HS 5603): BIS certification required under relevant IS standards.

## 5. ANTI-DUMPING AND COUNTERVAILING DUTIES

India's Directorate General of Trade Remedies (DGTR), under the Ministry of Commerce, has imposed Anti-Dumping Duty (ADD) and Countervailing Duties (CVD) on numerous textile products imported from China, Bangladesh, Vietnam, and other countries. As of the latest notifications:

### Key Anti-Dumping Duty Orders on Textiles
- **Polyester Staple Fibre (HS 5503.20)** from China, Korea, Taiwan: ADD in the range of USD 0.08 to USD 0.26 per kg.
- **Viscose Staple Fibre (HS 5504.10)** from Indonesia, China, Austria: ADD up to USD 0.19 per kg.
- **Nylon Tyre Cord Fabric (HS 5902.10)** from China: ADD of USD 0.90 per kg.
- **Woven Fabrics of Polyester (HS 5407)** from China: ADD in multiple review proceedings.
- **Denim Fabrics (HS 5209.42, 5211.42)** from China: ADD in force.
- **Readymade Garments (HS 6101–6117)** from Bangladesh: Exempt from ADD under SAFTA; eligible for 0% BCD subject to Rules of Origin compliance (minimum 30% value addition in Bangladesh).

**How ADD is applied at Customs**:
1. The Bill of Entry must declare the country of origin correctly.
2. CBIC officers verify the DGTR notification for applicable ADD rates.
3. ADD is levied in addition to BCD, SWS, and IGST on the assessable value.
4. Importers may apply to DGTR for Individual Duty Suspension if they can demonstrate higher prices than the floor price.

## 7. RULES OF ORIGIN FOR FTA TEXTILE IMPORTS

India's Free Trade Agreements impose specific **textile Rules of Origin (RoO)** to prevent trade deflection:

### ASEAN-India FTA (AIFTA)
- Garments (HS 61–62): **Yarn-Forward Rule** — yarn must be produced in ASEAN or India; fabric formation + garment manufacture must occur in ASEAN. Certificate of Origin Form AI required.
- Fabric: **Single Transformation Rule** acceptable for certain HS headings.

### South Asian Free Trade Agreement (SAFTA)
- Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal: Concessions for readymade garments subject to minimum value addition of 30% in the exporting country. COO Form SAFTA required.

### India-UAE CEPA (2022)
- Garments from UAE: 0% BCD subject to Rules of Origin compliance; Form ICEGC COO required.

## 8. MANDATORY DOCUMENT CHECKLIST FOR TEXTILE AND APPAREL IMPORTS

- [ ] **Bill of Entry (Form I)**: 8-digit HS code classification accuracy critical for duty calculation
- [ ] **Commercial Invoice**: Fabric composition declared (e.g., "60% cotton, 40% polyester"), GSM (grams per sq. metre), country of manufacture
- [ ] **Packing List**: Piece count, roll length, net/gross weight per bale or carton
- [ ] **Textile Committee Certificate / TRC**: For notified categories requiring TC inspection
- [ ] **Certificate of Origin (COO)**: Form AI (ASEAN), Form SAFTA, or non-preferential COO as applicable
- [ ] **Anti-Dumping Duty Compliance Declaration**: Country of origin declaration with supporting factory/manufacturer details
- [ ] **BIS Certification**: For technical textiles under BIS QCO notifications
- [ ] **Care Label Compliance**: Garments must bear washing instructions; labels in English or Hindi
- [ ] **Bill of Lading / Airway Bill**: Carrier document with container/ULD details
- [ ] **Country of Origin Certificate**: Verified by Chamber of Commerce or Trade Ministry of exporting country
